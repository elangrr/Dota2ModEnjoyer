# What is new

The newest version comes first. After an update, the app shows once what is new in it.

## 0.2.3

- A calmer, frosted glass look. On Windows 11 your desktop shows softly through the app, blurred. The buttons have a quieter colour, and the side bar and its icons are smaller. A computer that cannot draw it gets the plain look instead.
- Share your picks. "Share" on My picks or on a hero's page copies a short code. A friend pastes it under My picks, "Use a code", to get the same look. Right-click a preset to share it.
- Report a problem in two clicks. "Report" beside a message about something that went wrong, and "Report a problem" in Help, open a report on GitHub with the details already filled in. Your Windows name is left out of it.
- Effects of events and emblems picked with nothing picked for a hero showed on no hero, while the app said they were ready. Now the app says so, and one click gives them to every hero.
- A Dota update undoes half of the app's changes, which can stop you from finding a match. The app now puts such a game back to normal by itself whenever it is open, and Play puts your picks back. After an update, start Dota with Play in the app.
- If Dota ever says that Valve Anti-Cheat could not verify your machine, close Dota, press "Back to normal Dota", and look for a match again. Help says so too.
- If something goes wrong inside the app, it no longer freezes or closes without a word. It tells you, and offers to report it.
- Only one copy of the app opens at a time. Starting it again brings the open window to the front, so two copies can no longer undo each other's picks.
- When an antivirus or another program holds one of Dota's files for a moment, Play now waits and tries again. If it still cannot, it tells you in plain words what to do.
- Play no longer stops with an error when you pick the effects of Tinker's Mecha Boots of Travel Mk III together with the teleport of an event.
- Effects of events, such as a teleport, now show only on the heroes you chose. Before, choosing one of ten heroes that share a pet gave the effect to all ten.
- Profane Union now gives all of its Rage effects when you pick it under "Rage effects".
- Under an ability's effects, items whose look changes by itself during a fight, such as Bitter Lineage with Troll Warlord's stance, are now one choice instead of two.
- If Play stops halfway, for example because another program is using a game file, the app no longer says that your picks are in the game. Press Play again to finish.
- Kez and Largo can now be chosen in Settings, under "Effects of events and emblems".
- The "Dota 2 was not found" screen now tells you when a newer version of the app is out.
- When "Back to normal Dota" cannot be pressed, the app now tells you the real reason.
- From now on the Update button fills up like a circle while a new version downloads, and after each update the app shows what is new, like this list.

## 0.2.2

- A sun and moon switch at the bottom left chooses the day or night look.
- The ! mark for items with missing parts now also shows on sets, in search results and in My picks.
- Nyx Assassin's Tyrian Latticean Hierarchy gets back an effect that Dota keeps in the wrong folder.
- On the World page, Attack is now called Attack Projectile, and level badges are readable in the day look.

## 0.2.1

- The app's file is built in a different way, so that Windows Defender no longer mistakes it for malware. The file is larger for it. Nothing else changes.

## 0.2.0

- A new look, with a side bar of icons, glass panels, and Night and Day themes.
- A My picks page that lists everything you picked, with presets you can save and switch between.
- Typing now finds items and sets too.
- Rest the pointer on an item for a larger look.
- Filters for the hero grid.
- A logo, and an icon for the app.
- A Support page.
- The app now looks for updates every 5 minutes.

## 0.1.3

- Event effects and emblems now go only on the heroes you picked something for. Settings can change that.
- Low-end PC mode moved into Settings.
- Clearer warnings.

## 0.1.2

- The app finds new versions by itself from now on.
- Warnings on items that may not show fully, a Settings page, event Attack effects, courier effects on heroes, and personas on the hero's own items.

Versions before 0.1.2 do not look for updates by themselves.
